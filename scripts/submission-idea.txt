TokenGo is a location-based browser game where the loot is real AI compute. You walk your city and catch shards of Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, Gemini and Qwen that spawn around real landmarks (libraries favour Claude, stations GPT, parks Gemini) in a timing mini-game with combos and 25 collectible variants. Paris is the hand-built showcase; anywhere else the server maps your neighbourhood from OpenStreetMap on the fly, and from home you can fly a battery-limited courier pigeon (Remote Scout).

The economy is the gameplay. Every AI shopkeeper has its own stock, so its own price: walking from one café to another is arbitrage. Merchants shout their state in speech bubbles, pay rent, go bankrupt and get rescued by players; city events (flash crash, gold rush) shake prices for everyone; you can haggle in a short card game where "your rival pays more" only works if it is true. Every price has a "Why this price?" breakdown from one readable rules file, and an in-browser Economy Lab simulates a week of Paris with 200 bots in under a second using the exact live rules.

Forges on monuments compile daily recipes into real NEAR AI Cloud credits on the player's own API key: the only door from the game to real value, paid as a fraction of a capped reserve so the game is solvent by construction. Underneath, every item is a NEP-245 token on NEAR, settled in batches, and every real cent is preceded by an on-chain burn proof, but players never see a wallet, seed phrase or gas: guests play in seconds, judges get a pass link, and 24 runner bots keep the market alive around the clock.
